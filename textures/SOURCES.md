# Texture sources

All nine external PBR sets used in the scene are credited below, including CC0 work. `tools/import_textures.py` resizes, repacks and, where needed, colour-adjusts the maps into albedo / OpenGL normal / ORM (AO, roughness, height). Fabric061 is a procedural material from ambientCG; the Poly Haven surfaces are photoscans.
